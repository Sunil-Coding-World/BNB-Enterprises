FAQ

Aapke sawaal, hamare jawab.

Subsidy kitni milti hai aur kab aati hai?

PM Surya Ghar yojana mein 1 kW pe ₹30,000, 2 kW pe ₹60,000 aur 3 kW ya usse zyada pe ₹78,000 milti hai. Installation aur net-meter inspection ke baad subsidy seedha aapke bank account mein aati hai.

Mere ghar ke liye kitne kW ka system chahiye?

Generally 1 kW system mahine mein lagbhag 120 units banata hai. Upar wala calculator use kariye, ya free survey book kariye, hum exact size batayenge.

Installation mein kitna time lagta hai?

Booking ke baad registration aur approval mein 1–2 hafte, aur installation sirf 2–3 din. Net meter DISCOM lagata hai.

Kya EMI ya loan available hai?

Haan, kai nationalised banks rooftop solar pe kam interest ka loan dete hain. Hum documentation mein madad karte hain.

Maintenance kya karna padta hai?

Mahine mein 2 baar panel ko paani se saaf karna kaafi hai. Hamara AMC plan lene par ye kaam hamari team karti hai.
